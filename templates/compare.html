Methodology and notes

How these numbers are calculated

Normalisation. Each stock's closing price is divided by its first available close in the selected range and multiplied by 100. A value of 112 means a 12% gain from that starting point, regardless of the original price level.

Different calendars. The chart uses the union of all trading dates and forward-fills each stock. Statistics use only dates on which every selected stock traded, so returns are not calculated across stale prices.

Risk measure. Volatility is the sample standard deviation (ddof=1) of daily percentage returns on common dates. It is not annualized.

Volumes. Average volume uses common trading dates. Raw close values may be quoted in different currencies (INR for .NS/.BO, USD for US tickers); normalised performance avoids comparing those raw levels directly.
